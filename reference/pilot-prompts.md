# Pilot prompt pack · "In my town, everyone launches memecoins."

7 scenes. Each scene = one keyframe image (GPT Image, with the cast sheet + reference thumbnail attached) → one video clip (Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video, 720p, 9:16, from that keyframe + the cast sheet). Narration, one-word captions and the music loop are added on the server afterwards.

## Style block (goes at the top of every image prompt)

Vertical 9:16 in-game screenshot in the exact style of the reference images: classic Roblox-style block avatars (cylinder head with a flat top, block torso and straight block limbs, no hands or feet, flat 2D anime-eye face decals with eyebrows and a small mouth, hair meshes, matte plastic surfaces, even game lighting, no outlines). The world is built from simple game parts: flat-coloured blocks, wedges and cylinders, saturated colours, soft shadows, light rain, a faint overcast sky. Looks like a screenshot from a cute blocky online game, never like a Pixar film, never realistic, no fur, no fabric texture, no fingers. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI.

## Cast block (goes in every prompt, so every scene gets the same people)

- **Ollie (hero, always present):** beige skin, short messy brown hair mesh, small yellow bucket rain hat, bright yellow raincoat with three dark buttons and a hood, navy pants, navy boots. Face always visible, hat brim above the eyes. Expression: flat, calm, deadpan, eyebrows slightly raised. He usually holds a block smartphone with a cracked screen.
- **Milo:** light brown skin, black spiky hair mesh, round black goggles pushed up on his forehead, red scarf, green hoodie, grey shorts, white shoes. Expression: big open-mouth grin or big open-mouth shock.
- **Mrs. Pearl:** elderly, pale skin, grey hair in a bun, round glasses, purple knitted shawl over a dark navy dress. Hands clasped.
- **The Whale:** huge (two heads taller than everyone), white suit, thick gold chain, dark sunglasses, slicked black hair mesh, no expression.
- **The Callers:** three identical avatars in shiny black puffer jackets, each with a glowing white ring light strapped to the head like a halo.
- **The Bots:** plain grey block avatars with NO face decal (blank cylinder heads), identical, walking in a line.
- **The ducks:** white block-built ducks with orange beaks, tiny blue postal caps.

## World block

Candlewick, a blocky memecoin town. **The Flats:** flooded pastel block streets (mint, pink, pale yellow houses with white window frames) patched with brown cardboard, wooden plank docks, colourful block rowboats, little grey coin tokens floating in the water like fallen leaves, cracked phones lying around. **The Hill** above and behind: tall glass block towers with giant screens showing bright green rising charts, gold block fountains, rooftop pools. **The Launch Pad:** a concrete square between them with a huge red block button on a yellow base and a firework launcher; fireworks and little coin tokens shooting into the sky. **The Board:** a giant leaderboard screen over the square with ten gold name plates.

## Motion rules (go in every video prompt)

Keep every character's design identical to the keyframe and the cast sheet. Blocky, stiff, toy-like movement: avatars turn their whole body, heads rotate slowly, arms swing as rigid blocks, no finger movement, no cloth physics. The hero barely moves: stands still, blinks once, slowly turns his head to the camera. Slow smooth camera push-in, never a fast move. Leave the upper third of the frame clear for the caption. Ambient sound only (rain, distant fireworks, water), no music, no speech, no text.

## Scene 1 · 5 s
**Narration:** "In my town, everyone launches memecoins. Everyone."

**Keyframe:** Style block. The Launch Pad square at noon under a bright overcast sky. In the foreground, Ollie (cast block) stands centred, chest-up, facing the camera, deadpan, holding his cracked phone at his chest. Behind him and slightly to the right, a white block duck in a blue postal cap stands on top of the huge red block button, one wing raised, having just pressed it. A firework streak and three golden block coin tokens shoot up into the sky behind the duck, leaving thin white trails. Further back, a crowd of ten mixed block avatars (kids, a grandma, a dog in a hat) each hold up a phone toward the sky. Behind everything, the Hill's glass towers with green chart screens. Puddles on the concrete reflect the firework. Lighting: flat daylight, soft shadows. Composition: Ollie centred in the lower two thirds, the firework in the upper third but small, lots of sky.

**Video:** Motion rules. Ollie stands still and blinks once, then slowly turns his head a few degrees toward the camera. Behind him the duck hops off the button, the firework bursts quietly into a small golden sparkle high in the sky and the coin tokens tumble back down. The crowd's phones tilt up together. Light rain falls. Camera: slow push-in on Ollie over the whole clip. Audio: distant firework pop, rain, crowd murmur.

**Keyframe:** Style block. Wide establishing shot. Lower half: the Flats, a flooded pastel street with plank docks, two block rowboats, grey coin tokens floating everywhere, a cardboard-patched mint-green house, a cracked phone on a dock. Ollie (cast block) stands small on the nearest dock, back three-quarters to the camera, head tilted up. Upper half: the Hill rises behind, with four tall glass block towers, each with a huge screen showing a bright green chart going up, gold fountains at their base, a rooftop pool catching the light. A clear visual line between the two halves: the Hill is clean and glowing green, the Flats are wet and dim with warm window lights. Light rain. Rain ripples the water, a grey coin token drifts past the dock. The chart screens on the towers tick upward. Audio: rain, water lapping, a faint electronic chime from the Hill.

## Scene 3 · 5 s
**Narration:** "My best friend Milo launched a coin this morning. It's already at zero."

**Keyframe:** Style block. A plank dock in the Flats, midday. Milo (cast block) stands centre-left holding his cracked block phone out toward the camera with both block arms; the phone screen shows a simple chart: a short green spike then a flat red line along the bottom. His mouth is a wide open block "O" of shock, goggles pushed up. Ollie (cast block) stands centre-right, half a step back, hands at his sides, completely deadpan, looking at the camera, not at the phone. Behind them a pink block house with a cardboard-patched window, a red rowboat, grey coins in the water, a duck in a postal cap watching from a post. Composition: the two kids chest-up in the lower two thirds, phone screen clearly readable.

**Video:** Motion rules. Milo's arms shake slightly as he holds the phone out; his head turns from the phone to Ollie and back, stiffly. Ollie does not move except one slow blink. The duck turns its head. Rain. Camera: very slow push-in. Audio: rain, a tiny sad "beep" from the phone, water.

## Scene 4 · 5 s
**Narration:** "I have a thing I don't talk about. When I look at a chart, the winning trade glows green."

**Keyframe:** Style block. Close-up on Ollie's face (cast block), sunset, filling the lower two thirds of the frame, the hat brim just above his eyes. He holds his cracked phone up at chin height, slightly below the camera, so its light shines up onto his face. On the phone screen a candlestick chart of small grey candles; ONE candle in the middle glows an intense bright green with a soft glow halo around it. That same green glow reflects as a small bright highlight in Ollie's left eye. His expression is calm, mouth closed, one eyebrow slightly up. Background: a blurred Flats street at sunset, orange sky, warm window lights, rain streaks. Composition: face centred, phone in the lower left, the upper third is soft sky.

**Video:** Motion rules. The single green candle pulses brighter once; the reflection in Ollie's eye brightens with it. Ollie's eyes shift very slowly from the phone to the camera. Nothing else moves except rain. Camera: slow push-in on his face. Audio: rain, a single soft chime when the candle pulses.

## Scene 5 · 5 s
**Narration:** "If the KOLs find out, I'm gone. So I lose on purpose. In public."

**Keyframe:** Style block. The Launch Pad square at sunset. Above, the giant screen on a Hill tower shows a Caller's face (shiny black puffer jacket, glowing ring light halo) with a green arrow beside it. A duck in a postal cap stands at the edge. Wet concrete reflects the big screen. Composition: Ollie centred chest-up, the Caller on the screen in the upper third, kids on both sides.

**Video:** Motion rules. The kids' heads bob as they laugh, stiffly. Ollie's shrug finishes and his arms lower slowly; he blinks once at the camera. The Caller on the screen points down at the crowd. Rain. Camera: slow push-in on Ollie. Audio: kids laughing, rain, a distant firework.

## Scene 6 · 5 s
**Narration:** "At night I trade alone. My wallet is bigger than the Whale's. Nobody knows."

**Keyframe:** Style block. Ollie's small bedroom at night, nearly dark, lit only by a green glow from the floor. Ollie (cast block) kneels on the wooden plank floor, hat still on, and has lifted one floorboard with both block arms. Under the floorboard a block phone lies face-up, its screen glowing bright green with a chart going up and up, and a long number. The green light lights his face from below, so his eyes and the underside of the hat brim are lit green while the rest of the room is dark blue. Behind him a block bed with a yellow blanket, a window with rain streaks and a faint red glow from the Flats outside, a poster on the wall of a block rocket. Composition: Ollie centred in the lower two thirds, the glowing phone at the bottom, the dark upper third mostly empty wall.

**Video:** Motion rules. Ollie holds the floorboard still; the green chart on the phone ticks up twice and the glow brightens with each tick. He slowly turns his head to look over his shoulder at the window. Rain on the glass. Camera: slow push-in from slightly above. Audio: rain on the window, two tiny chart ticks, floorboard creak.

## Scene 7 · 6 s (the cliffhanger)
**Narration:** "Tonight, one of the Whale's Bots stopped outside my window. It turned its head."

**Keyframe:** Style block. Shot from inside Ollie's dark bedroom, looking out through the rain-streaked block window. Outside, under a single street lamp in the flooded street, a Bot (cast block: plain grey block avatar, blank cylinder head, no face) stands completely still in the rain, water up to its block ankles, its head turned to face the window directly. In the foreground at the right edge of the frame, Ollie (cast block) stands pressed beside the curtain, only half of him visible, frozen, eyes wide, lit faintly green from the floor below. The room is dark blue; the lamp outside is warm yellow; the Bot is dead grey. Grey coin tokens float in the street water. Composition: the Bot centred in the window in the middle of the frame, Ollie at the right edge, the top third is dark window frame and sky.

**Video:** Motion rules. For the first three seconds nothing moves except rain. Then the Bot's blank head rotates slowly, stiffly, a few degrees further toward the camera, and stops. Ollie's eyes shift toward the Bot; he does not move otherwise. The street lamp flickers once. Camera: very slow push-in toward the window. Audio: rain, one lamp buzz, silence.
