# BloxTV · Season 1 bible: "The Flats"

The one story the show tells. The writer model gets this every episode; the offline fallback follows it too.

## The town: Candlewick

A blocky town built around one thing: launching memecoins. Everyone does it. Kids, grandmas, the ducks.

- **The Hill.** Glass towers, gold block fountains, rooftop pools. The KOLs live here. Every tower has a giant screen showing a green chart, always green.
- **The Flats.** Flooded streets, plank docks, pastel houses patched with cardboard. Retail lives here. Everyone owns a boat and a phone with a cracked screen. The water is full of dead coins, little grey tokens floating like leaves.
- **The Launch Pad.** A concrete square between the Hill and the Flats with a big red button. Press it, a coin launches, a firework goes up. Fireworks all day, all night.
- **The Board.** A leaderboard screen over the square showing the top ten wallets in town. Only Hill names on it. Nobody from the Flats has ever been on it.
- **Night.** Charts glow through every window. The Flats go red. The Hill stays green.

## The rules of Ollie's power ("the green eye")

1. When Ollie looks at a chart, the winning trade glows green for one second. Only he sees it.
2. It never shows how much. Only which.
3. **If anyone sees him win, the eye goes dark for seven days.** So he trades alone, at night, and loses on purpose in public.
4. Nobody can know. The Whale made his fortune on something like it, and he keeps a list of everyone who wins too often.

## The cast

- **Ollie** (narrator, 12). Yellow raincoat, bucket hat, deadpan. Lives in the Flats with his grandma. Acts broke. Secretly the richest wallet in town, hidden under a floorboard.
- **Milo** (best friend, 12). Goggles, red scarf, green hoodie. Loud, hopeful, launches a coin every week. Every one goes to zero. Wants to be a KOL more than anything.
- **Mrs. Pearl** (next door, old). Purple shawl, round glasses. Used to live on the Hill. Knows what the Whale is and won't say why she left. Has a photo she never shows.
- **The Whale** (the big KOL). A huge block avatar in a white suit and gold chain, dark sunglasses, never seen below the Hill. Owns the Board. Everyone in town has bought one of his coins. Everyone has lost.
- **The Callers** (his crew, three of them). Identical KOLs in puffer jackets with ring lights on their heads. They "call" coins on the Hill screens. They always buy before they call.
- **The Bots.** Grey faceless block avatars that walk in lines through the Flats and buy whatever the Callers call. They never speak. They work for the Whale.
- **The ducks.** Run the post office. Deliver airdrops. Strict. (They stay from the old town because people liked them.)

Season 2 starts with the Whale's offer, Ollie's eye going dark, and Milo coming home.
